Proposed Initial Implementation Model

DRONEXA America Rural Nebraska Drone-Integrated STEM Pilot

A proposed research-to-practice implementation and evidence-development model designed to strengthen educator capacity, expand access to emerging-technology learning, support inclusive participation, and connect K–12 STEM experiences with an evolving UAS workforce.

Purpose

From developed resources to real-world educational evidence.

The Rural Nebraska Pilot is being developed as an initial environment in which selected DRONEXA America resources and approaches could be implemented, evaluated, refined, and documented.

The proposed pilot is intended to connect research and resource development with educator preparation, student learning experiences, inclusive mission design, implementation feedback, and evidence that can inform future improvement and adaptation.

Why Rural Nebraska?

A setting for examining access, capacity, and implementation beyond major metropolitan areas.

Rural schools can offer an important environment for examining how emerging-technology STEM opportunities can be introduced where geography, staffing, specialized resources, professional learning access, and local capacity may shape implementation differently.

Demonstration, not geographic limitation

Nebraska is envisioned as an initial evidence-development environment. The broader DRONEXA America model is intended to be adaptable to other educational settings as evidence, resources, and implementation experience develop.

Proposed Initial Region

A focused starting point for implementation planning.

The proposed initial implementation area is centered within the Educational Service Unit 10 (ESU 10) service region in central Nebraska, with an initial focus on school communities in Custer and Dawson Counties.

This geographic focus is intended to provide a manageable setting for stakeholder engagement, implementation planning, educator preparation, school-level implementation, evaluation, and refinement before consideration of broader adaptation.

Geographic focus, not institutional commitment

Identification of this proposed geographic area does not indicate that ESU 10 or any school district within the region has agreed to participate in, endorse, fund, implement, or formally partner with DRONEXA America.

Proposed Implementation Sequence

Four phases from planning to evidence development

Phase I

Stakeholder Engagement & Planning

Engage relevant stakeholders, identify local priorities and implementation conditions, explore potential roles and participation, assess resource and readiness considerations, and collaboratively refine an appropriate implementation structure.

Phase II

Educator Preparation

Prepare an initial educator cohort through professional learning, safety and regulatory orientation, instructional planning, and use of DRONEXA resources.

Phase III

School Implementation

Implement selected curriculum-aligned drone STEM missions and gather structured feedback on learning, access, feasibility, and educator experience.

Phase IV

Regional Collaboration & Evidence Development

Support shared learning, student opportunities, documentation, evaluation, refinement, and development of evidence for future adaptation.

Evaluation & Evidence Development

Implementation is intended to generate evidence for improvement.

Evaluation would focus not only on whether activities occurred, but on what can be learned from implementation and how the model should change.

01

Educator Readiness

Changes in knowledge, confidence, planning capacity, and perceived implementation feasibility.

02

Student Engagement & Learning

Evidence of participation, applied STEM learning, collaboration, and skill development.

03

Inclusive Participation

How effectively mission structures and supports create meaningful access and contribution.

04

Implementation Feasibility

Time, equipment, staffing, facilities, scheduling, and other conditions affecting practical use.

05

Safety & Responsible Practice

How safety procedures, supervision, ethics, and regulatory awareness function during implementation.

06

Continuous Improvement

Feedback and evidence used to refine resources, professional learning, missions, and implementation guidance.

Current Status

A proposed model under development.

The Rural Nebraska Pilot is currently a proposed implementation model. Its scope, schedule, participating institutions, resource requirements, responsibilities, and implementation details would be determined before implementation.

Public-status statement

This page describes the proposed structure and objectives of the pilot. It does not represent a claim that any school, district, educational agency, organization, or other institution has agreed to participate, partner, endorse, fund, or implement the initiative.
