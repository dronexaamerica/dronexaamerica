DRONEXA America welcomes thoughtful dialogue and professional input from people and organizations whose expertise can strengthen the initiative as its frameworks, resources, and implementation approaches continue to develop.

Why Collaborate?

Strong educational innovation develops through multiple forms of expertise.

Drone-integrated STEM education sits at the intersection of teaching and learning, emerging technology, aviation safety, accessibility, workforce preparation, policy, institutional practice, and community context.

DRONEXA America is therefore being developed through a research-to-practice approach that values professional review, educator experience, stakeholder perspective, implementation evidence, and continuous refinement.

A Development-Stage Initiative

Collaboration should be described accurately.

DRONEXA America is actively developing its principal frameworks, resources, and implementation approaches. Conversations during this stage are intended to strengthen the work and explore possibilities—not to presume institutional commitment.

Collaboration status

An inquiry, conversation, review request, exchange of information, or expression of interest should not be interpreted as a formal partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, adoption, funding commitment, or implementation agreement unless such a relationship is expressly established.
