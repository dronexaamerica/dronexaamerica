Advancing America’s Drone Workforce Through Education

DRONEXA America is an educational initiative being developed to support the safe, inclusive, standards-aligned, and workforce-oriented integration of unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) into K–12 education.

Why DRONEXA America

From research to practice. From classrooms to careers.

DRONEXA America translates research on drone technology integration in K–12 STEM education into practical frameworks, educator resources, implementation models, and evidence-building initiatives.

Our Work

Five connected priorities

Rural STEM Access

Expanding opportunities for rural and underserved communities to engage with emerging drone technologies.

Educator Capacity Building

Developing research-informed guidance, professional learning, and implementation support for educators.

Inclusive Drone-STEM Education

Advancing meaningful participation through Flight Without Limits™ and accessible learning design.

Workforce & Career Pathways

Connecting K–12 learning with technical skills, career awareness, and America's evolving drone ecosystem.

Safety & Regulatory Readiness

Promoting responsible UAS learning grounded in safety, regulatory awareness, and ethical technology use.

Signature Inclusive STEM Component

Flight Without Limits™

Flight Without Limits™ is an inclusive UAS-STEM framework currently in development. It is being designed to support meaningful participation of students with mobility impairments and other physical accessibility needs.

Designed around

  • Accessible learning environments
  • Multiple mission roles
  • Assistive technology considerations
  • Adaptable participation pathways
  • Inclusive mission design

Initial Implementation

Rural Nebraska Drone-Integrated STEM Pilot

The proposed Rural Nebraska pilot is being developed as an initial implementation and evidence-development environment for DRONEXA America. The model is intended to be refined through educator feedback, evaluation, documentation, and future adaptation.
